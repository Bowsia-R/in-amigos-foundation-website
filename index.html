Our Story, Our Mission!

InAmigos Foundation, founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh. Our mission is to create a positive impact in society by supporting underprivileged communities and promoting social welfare. With 80G, 12A, CSR-1, NITI Aayog registration and ISO 9001:2015 certification, we are committed to transparency, accountability and quality service.



~ Our Initiatives ~

At InAmigos Foundation, we believe that every small effort can create a meaningful impact. Our initiatives are designed to address important social and environmental challenges while empowering individuals and communities to build a better future. From providing essential support to underprivileged families and quality education to children, to protecting animals, empowering women, conserving nature and enhancing employability, we strive to bring positive change across India.

Through the dedication of our volunteers, professionals and supporters, we work together to create opportunities, spread awareness and promote kindness, equality and sustainability. Each project reflects our commitment to building a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society.


Project Bachpanshala 📚

Project Bachpanshala focuses on providing quality education to underprivileged children. Through learning opportunities, educational support and skill development, we aim to empower children with knowledge, confidence and a brighter future.



Project Seva 🤝

Project Seva focuses on supporting underprivileged communities by providing essential needs such as food and clothing. Through the dedication of volunteers and supporters, we aim to bring hope, dignity and happiness to those in need.



Project Jeev 🐾

Project Jeev focuses on animal welfare through rescue, protection and feeding. We work to provide care and support to animals in need, promoting kindness and compassion towards all living beings.



Project Prakriti 🌿

Project Prakriti focuses on environmental conservation and sustainability. Through awareness, eco-friendly practices and efforts to protect nature, we aim to create a cleaner, greener and healthier planet for future generations.
